Floor / RFC-014 / Input experiment

One million rows. Owned touch.

A standalone prototype of logical scrolling with signed touch velocity and a viewport-sized content box. Drag, fling, catch, change direction, and interrupt a smooth jump.

Experimental boundary policy: same-axis touch stops at either edge. It does not hand off to the page—even when a new gesture starts inside the list. Swipe the page margins to scroll the parent. This behavior is under evaluation, not approved for v3.
