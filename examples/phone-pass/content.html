Phone pass

Five things that only a real finger on a real phone can answer. Work down the page. Each card says what to do and what should happen, and reports what it actually measured while you did it.

1

Long press starts a drag, and nothing else

Do: press and hold a row for about a second, then drag it up or down and let go.

Should: the row lifts and follows your finger. No text gets selected, no magnifier appears, and no copy/paste or share menu pops up.
